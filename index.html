signalbot-rclone

signalbot-rclone is a private backup tool used by one person, its owner, on his own computers. It uses rclone to copy his own files into his own Google Drive and to read them back to check that the copy is intact.

It asks Google only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and change only the files it created itself, and nothing else in the Drive.

It is not offered to anyone else, and it has no users other than its owner.
